# Bug Report Template

## Title

Some buttons on the Login page don't show a "hand" cursor when you hover over them

## Summary

Normally, when you move your mouse over something clickable, the cursor changes to a little hand to show it's clickable. On the Login page, three items don't do this: the eye icon that shows/hides the password, the "Log In" button, and the "Forgot Password?" text.

## Description

### Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Login page.
2. Move the mouse over the eye icon inside the password box.
3. Move the mouse over the "Log In" button.
4. Move the mouse over the "Forgot Password?" text.

### Actual Result

The mouse cursor stays as a regular arrow over all three items, even though they can be clicked.

### Expected Result

The mouse cursor should change to a hand/pointer over any item that can be clicked.

## Severity

Low

## Priority

P3
