# Bug Report Template

## Title

"Welcome Back" title is too big

## Summary

The "Welcome Back" text at the top of the login form is much larger than everything else on the page, making it feel out of balance with the rest of the design.

## Description

### Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Login page.
2. Look at the "Welcome Back" title compared to the rest of the form (labels, box text, button text).

### Actual Result

"Welcome Back" is roughly 4 times bigger than the other text on the page, taking up a large amount of space at the top.

### Expected Result

"Welcome Back" should be sized more in line with the rest of the page. The exact size should be confirmed with the design team, since there isn't currently an agreed size to check against.

## Severity

Low

## Priority

P3
